Hi, I'm Anthony.

I'm a freshman at Brown University studying Electrical Engineering and Economics — a combination I chose deliberately because I believe the most impactful work sits at the intersection of deep technical fluency and financial clarity.

This summer, I'm simultaneously working as an Investment Banking Summer Analyst at Network 1 Financial Securities, a Machine Vision Engineering Intern at Optopax, and a Research Associate at Brown's PROBE Lab. I thrive operating across these contexts: modeling M&A transactions by day, debugging inference pipelines in the evening, and advancing biomedical hardware research on weekends.

I'm drawn to high-stakes, ambiguous problems — the kind where you have to synthesize technical rigor with strategic judgment. Whether that's benchmarking Nasdaq listing criteria for a space company, training object-detection models on custom datasets, or improving pulse oximetry accuracy for under-represented skin tones, I bring the same energy: structured, fast, and outcome-driven.

Outside work, I'm the Family Chair for Brown-RISD VSA, a consultant at the Sustainable Business Society, and spend my free time playing chess, DJing, or hunting for a new fragrance.
